**Upgrade notes:**
- Core stays at 0.84.0 and the control-plane protocol at 55. Nothing is cleared.

**Overview:**
- What used to sit at the top of the overview now lives in the notices (the bell). Failed requests and models without a price are new notices there, shown in the app only; a cut-off tool call and an unavailable upstream were notices already. Clicking the failed-requests notice shows only the failed requests in Traffic.
- The usage chart draws lines instead of bars. Tokens, cost, requests and traffic are shaded areas; failures and downloads are a second, thin line. The period still in progress is dashed, and periods from before the first request record are marked "Not recorded" instead of being drawn as zero.
- The chart's scale fits the data more closely: a peak of 3.9M now reads 0-5M instead of 0-8M, and axis labels drop the trailing ".0".
- Clicking a day on the past-year heatmap, or changing the time range further down the page, no longer jumps back to the top. The heatmap stays where it was.
- Hovering the chart no longer lights up the border of the whole card.
- Distribution and Breakdown follow one choice of model, upstream or key. Distribution has its title and a slim bar per row, and Breakdown sits in its own titled card.
- The past-year heatmap uses calmer colors, its totals are a size smaller than the metrics above, and days before recording started are faint cells instead of dashed boxes.
- When there is no previous period to compare with, the metric tiles leave the change line empty instead of showing a dash.
- The overview opens on the last 24 hours until a time range has been chosen.
