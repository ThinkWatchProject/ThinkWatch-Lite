**Upgrade notes:**
- The bundled core moves to 0.84.0 and the control-plane protocol to 55. A remote server running an older core needs core 0.84.0 or later before this version can connect to it.
- Nothing is cleared; the request history, stored payloads and settings carry over.
- Core now keeps daily usage in its own file, apart from the request records, so it is not removed with them. On first start it is filled from the request records already kept.

**Overview:**
- Usage: the six small cards are replaced by six metric tabs (tokens, cost, requests, cache hits, first token, traffic) over one large chart with axes. Hovering a bar shows that period and its top models. Cost tiles keep their qualifiers, such as estimated amounts, in view.
- Distribution: tokens, cost or requests by model, upstream or key, following the selected metric. Rows show model and service logos.
- Quotas and balances: subscription windows and balances sit beside the distribution when an upstream reports them.
- Past year: a day-by-day heatmap of tokens, cost or requests, with the total, daily average, busiest day and streaks. Clicking a day shows that day in the overview. Days older than the oldest request record cannot be opened, because their details are gone; hovering says so.
- The status line shows the generation speed while requests are running.

**Fixes:**
- Settings > Log retention > Payload storage in use: a size just under 1 MB or 1 GB reads "1 MB" or "1 GB" instead of "1024 KB" or "1024 MB".
- Quota and key-limit notices round the reset time before picking a unit, so 1 h 59 min reads "about 2 hours", matching the menu bar.

**Core 0.84.0:**
- Updates the zstd compression library to a release that fixes four security advisories.
