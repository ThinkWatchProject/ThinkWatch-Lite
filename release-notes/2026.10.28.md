**Upgrade notes:**
- The bundled core is now 0.81.0. The control-plane protocol stays at 51, so a remote server running core 0.80.0 still connects; the behavior described below needs 0.81.0 on the side that runs the gateway.
- Nothing is cleared; the request history, stored payloads and settings carry over.

**Conversations with encrypted history stay on their upstream:**
- OpenAI Responses upstreams, such as a ChatGPT account, hand clients encrypted history that only the same account can read. Codex sends it back every turn. Before, moving Codex's route to another upstream made a conversation under way fail on every turn.
- Such a conversation now goes back to the upstream that encrypted its history, even after its route changes. The request details show this under Conversation.
- If that upstream was removed, or another upstream is asked to read the history, the request fails with a message that explains why and suggests routing the conversation back or starting a new one.

**Routing:**
- In the route table's rule list, the letter tile shown for an upstream without a logo lines up with the text.
