This release improves the auxiliary request and web-tool controls under **Routing → Auxiliary**.

- Handling controls align at the right edge of the table. Web-tool Configure and Test buttons sit beside the tool name and wrap when space is limited.
- Limits and cache settings have a full-width expandable heading with a direction indicator and keyboard support.

This release retains Core 0.98.0 and the Google (Docs), DuckDuckGo and Bing search support introduced in 2026.11.17.
